## 1. Shrnutí pro vlastníka (česky)

Bezpečnostní audit potvrdil skutečné, dnes zneužitelné díry: administrátor organizace může pozvat vlastníka na nižší roli a "shodit" ho z účtu; kdokoli se znalostí ID uživatele ho může bez pozvání přidat do své organizace a číst jeho e-mail; herní panel (Pterodactyl) párujeme podle e-mailu, který si zákazník sám nastaví, takže lze převzít cizí herní server a resetovat mu heslo; na sdíleném aaPanelu jde symlinkem uniknout z webového adresáře a číst/psát cizí soubory jako root; staffové globální role (např. podpora) dosáhnou i na zákaznická práva a mohou mazat data bez čtyř očí; partnerský výplatní systém nemá zámek proti dvojímu vyplacení a IBAN lze přepsat bez dalšího ověření.

Navržený model: jeden bod vynucení (CommandBus), rozlišení režimu zákazník/staff (staffové už nedosáhnou na zákaznická práva přes globální roli), tvrdá hranice tenantů (žádný přístup bez výslovného, časově omezeného, odvolatelného souhlasu), riziko se nikdy nesnižuje (CRITICAL = druhý schvalovatel, u samostatného provozovatele časová pojistka místo obejití), a každá akce patří do jedné ze 4 tříd obnovitelnosti (kopie+undo, inverzní příkaz, grace/archiv, nebo čestně "nelze vrátit").

Okamžité opravy (dnes zneužitelné, důkazy v kódu): zámek vlastníka organizace; zákaz vstupu do organizace bez pozvání; ověření herního panelu podle organizace, ne e-mailu; uzavření symlink/root-API díry na aaPanelu; oddělení stafferských a zákaznických práv; zámek na výplaty partnerů.

Fázový plán: P0 (bezpečnostní záplaty, git larga PR #24), Slice 1 (přístupový wizard pro zákazníky/subuživatele), Slice 2 (podpora vázaná na tiket + undo), Slice 3 (partneři/reselleři), Slice 4 (JIT/break-glass), Slice 5 (panely, audit, infrastruktura).

Co nelze vrátit: doména po transferu, odeslaná pošta, platby/refundy, reset panelového heslo, smazání herní databáze — u těchto akcí místo slibu "undo" nabízíme jasné varování, vyšší úroveň rizika a náhradu (registrar redemption, kde existuje).

Otázky pro vás jsou na konci dokumentu (9 rozhodnutí s výchozí volbou, kterou beru, pokud neřeknete jinak).

Nejsem schopen slíbit nulový bezpečnostní defekt — jen měřitelně snížené a testované riziko s trvalým revizním programem.

## 2. Principles

1. **One enforcement point.** Every write goes through the CommandBus — invitation acceptance, hooks, Discord, staff SSO, partner/support grants, undo. It authorizes the real actor at a scope loaded from stored rows (never the payload), persists the full authorization basis on the operation, and fails closed on anything unmapped.
2. **Authorization is mode-aware.** Customer mode counts only organization/project/resource bindings of the organization being acted on. Staff mode (set only by `/v1/staff/*` middleware, persisted) counts global/family/JIT bindings, and only for `audience=staff` permissions. No staff role holds a customer-audience permission.
3. **A token or linked credential is a narrowed principal view.** It sees only its own organization's bindings, never global bindings, and never grants.
4. **One GrantPolicy decides every grant, change and removal.** A grantor gives only what it effectively holds at that scope or an ancestor. Nobody changes someone they do not cover. Grants never outlive the grantor. The owner binding changes only through a two-sided transfer.
5. **The tenant boundary is hard.** Attribution, referral, shared e-mail, and panel-side identity grant nothing. Cross-tenant access exists only as an explicit, scoped, expiring, revocable grant naming individual people, audited on both sides.
6. **Risk only goes up.** Effective risk = max(declared, catalogue). HIGH needs a fresh step-up of the real actor. CRITICAL needs a second person; where only one approver exists, a time lock (delay + notice + cancel) replaces silent bypass.
7. **Honest recoverability.** Every action belongs to exactly one class: A (platform pre-copy + undo), B (inverse command), C (grace/soft-delete + final archive), D (externally irreversible → preview + confirm + higher risk). Undo never restores revoked access and never silently discards other people's later writes.
8. **Copies live out of the tenant's reach.** Pre-copies and file versions sit in platform storage outside the docroot, verified by hash, pruned at the promised retention unless under legal hold.
9. **Panels are not trusted to enforce tenancy.** The platform proves ownership of every panel identity before acting; where a panel cannot isolate tenants (multi-tenant aaPanel), code execution and file writes are closed there instead of engineered around.
10. **Identifiers are scoped and audited.** Idempotency keys are namespaced `org:service:action:actor`; audit rows carry resource, correlation id, session/token identity and state hashes.
11. **Existing customers are never changed en masse silently.** Every tightening ships with a default-off switch or a `--dry-run`/`--apply` operator command plus notice. New meaning gets a new preset key, never a silent edit of an existing one.
12. **Catalogue and hot-file changes ship expand/contract, serially, in lanes**, one small PR each, merged with `--merge`. Evidence before claims: a failing-first test per exploit, an independent read-only review closing every phase.

## 3. Target model

**Principals & views:** User (customer or staff-eligible), ServiceAccount, ApiToken (user + required `organization_id` + scopes + expiry + optional resource/IP allow-list), LinkedCredential (Discord/hook — organization-bound, expiring), PartnerOrganization. A `PrincipalView = (principal, mode: customer|staff, tokenOrganizationId?, delegationId?, supportGrantId?)` is built per request and persisted on operations/approvals.

**Scopes:** global > family (staff only) > organization > project > resource(service) > sub-resource (proven by ownership check, not new bindings). Coverage rules: organization/project/resource bindings count only in customer mode for `audience=customer` permissions; global/family/JIT bindings count only in staff mode for `audience=staff` permissions; token/linked-credential views ignore global bindings and any binding outside their own organization.

**Permissions & roles:** `PermissionCatalog` is the single list (key, audience, risk, family, token scope, localized cs/en sentence). `RoleResolver` is the only role→permission mapper (an architecture test forbids reading the raw catalogue elsewhere); unknown keys resolve to nothing and fail closed. Staff roles lose every `audience=customer` permission and gain `staff.*` equivalents (staff.backup.restore/delete, staff.billing.read, **staff.support.ticket.read**, staff.service.read/manage/delete [CRITICAL], staff.console). Customer presets (owner, org_admin, billing_admin, domain_manager, dns_manager, developer, cloud_operator, game_operator, mail_manager, security_auditor, support_contact, viewer, guest) are unchanged; `partner` is relabelled "Partner (commission only)". A closed **family × level matrix** (`fam:<family>:<level>`, family = web|mail|vps|game|domain_dns|billing, level = view|operate|manage|code|console) gives customers granularity without free-form custom roles. New service presets: `svc_operate` (wizard default: restart, PHP version, cache, SSL, logs — no file writes/cron), `svc_manage` (unchanged, relabelled "can run code"), `svc_data_delete` (new, gates destructive per-object deletes). Free custom roles are deferred (owner question, §10 O6); if built later they extend the existing `roles`/`role_permissions` tables with `organization_id` + version, never a second store.

**GrantPolicy invariants (I1–I12), apply to every grant kind:** grantor effectively holds every granted permission at the target scope or an ancestor (I1); grantee ≠ grantor except accepting/lowering your own (I2); grantor covers the target's *current* effective permissions (I3); `organization.owner_user_id` always holds an active owner binding, changed only by two-step transfer (I4); `expires_at` ≤ grantor's own expiry (I5); grantor loss cancels pending grants and flags active dependents (I6); adding HIGH/CRITICAL or an org-scope membership change is HIGH, batches need one step-up (I7); `change_role`/`remove`/project changes target current members only, the only way in is `AcceptInvitationCommand` (I8); accepting never lowers an existing membership (I9); an access snapshot precedes every removal/role change, kept 90 days, restorable (I10); project roles come from an allow-list (I11); reshare depth ≤ 2, within the resharer's own rights (I12).

**Staff & support:** `staffMode` set only by `/v1/staff/*` middleware, persisted in `operations.context` and the approval payload hash. `STAFF_PERMISSIONS` maps action→staff permission→tier (L1 read/safe, L2 manage, L3 console; deletes = CRITICAL). Every staff read/write of customer data needs an access basis: (a) **ticket-bound consent** — only on a *portal-authenticated* customer-originated ticket (first message from a current member via the portal/API, never an inbound e-mail/phone claim) about service X; view is preselected, changes opt-in, ends 72h after ticket close; org setting ask-per-ticket (default) | always-view | never; a standalone `SupportAccessGrant` (level read|operate|console, ≤7d) covers non-ticket cases; or (b) **incident basis** (abuse|legal|payment|security) with reason ≥10 chars + ticket_ref — reads HIGH, class-B writes (suspend/quarantine) HIGH with immediate notice + 24h post-review, destructive/console/export CRITICAL. Sales/account-manager access (a distinct "prodejce" role from resellers) uses a portfolio-assignment basis: read-only commercial data, no service/ticket content, no contact export. Staff SSO = `PanelLoginCommand`, HIGH, requires a console-level basis of the same family. JIT: staff role keys only, approver ≠ requester, TTL ≤8h, ticket/family/org scope, expiry sweep. Break-glass: `platform_owner` reachable only via CRITICAL/time-locked activation or offline CLI, with TTL, IAM-wide alert, mandatory post-review.

**Four-eyes & the solo operator:** CRITICAL needs an approver ≠ requester holding the decider permission. The single-operator waiver applies only when the actor is the sole holder of `iam.approval.decide`; their own CRITICAL action then goes through a **time lock** (default 24h, notify + cancel), never a silent bypass. Everyone else always gets an Approval. Doctor flags red when the waiver is on and other CRITICAL-capable actors exist without the time lock.

**Panels:** a provenance ledger records `creator_principal_id`/`service_id` for every platform-created panel identity; revocation works by creator, not e-mail. A per-service **revocation epoch** makes restore/undo re-run artefact cleanup and flag "credentials possibly exposed" with one-click rotation (limited to platform-known artefacts — in-application backdoors such as hand-added SSH keys inside a restored copy are a stated residual risk). ISPConfig: one `onh_` client per org, ownership proven by prefix + `system_group`/`document_root` + a stored `client_id` binding. aaPanel: multi-tenant nodes are closed to customer file writes/shell cron (`operator:aapanel:tenancy --dry-run/--apply`); new shared-web sales go to ISPConfig or single-tenant aaPanel; archives are pre-listed and refused on symlink/hardlink/absolute/`..` entries; remaining writes run as the site user, no-follow. Pterodactyl: keep the raw-orgId `external_id` convention, look up by exact `GET /users/external/{orgId}` and require the returned `external_id` to equal `service.organization_id`; new users get a synthetic e-mail; sub-users use a capability allow-list, mandatory expiry, and a reconciliation sweep that only removes platform-created, unbacked ones.

**Recoverability (RecoveryRegistry, classes A–D):** every `ServiceActionCommand` action is classified; unclassified fails an architecture test. A = platform pre-copy + `OperationUndoCommand` (files-only or full-set copy, DB dump, zone version, VM vzdump-off-node or 72h-TTL snapshot). B = inverse command. C = grace/soft-delete + final archive (mailbox delete disabled-then-purged; domain deletion classified per TLD/registrar, defaulting to D unless redemption is verified). D = external-irreversible (transfer-out, DS publish, sent mail, payments/refunds/payouts, panel password reset, `gamedb.delete/rotate`) → preview + confirm + ≥HIGH, CRITICAL for money out. `UndoEligibility`: your own operation needs the original permission + step-up; another's needs `backup.restore` + GrantPolicy coverage of that person; never undoes security/abuse/revocation/system actions; refused after a newer revocation epoch until cleanup re-runs. Default restore is non-mirror; mirror is opt-in and itself pre-copied. Retention stays at the promised value (default 60d, floor 30d); prune removes protected `pre_*` rows and snapshots after `retention_until` unless under legal hold.

**Partners/resellers/affiliates:** affiliate/referral = rewards only, masked names, no access, one reward per registration (partner wins on conflict). Partner attribution grants no access; contact/dunning data is masked unconditionally, with recorded client consent levels (none|aggregate|contact) shown and revocable in the client portal. Commission uses a normalised-MRR base, is *pending* until the withdrawal/chargeback window closes, then payable, clawed back on credit-note/chargeback/refund-to-credit, and frozen while the partner is suspended. Payout: row-locked, `amount = sum(allocated)` computed inside the transaction, paid only from `approved` by a person different from the approver; the IBAN comes from a verified, owner-only, HIGH, 7-day-cooling-off payout account (existing IBANs already used for a paid payout are grandfathered as confirmed). Reseller MVP: the partner creates the client organization; the client's invitation acceptance carries a plain-language consent; a `DelegationGrant` (scope org|project|services, level per family, named partner users visible to the client) never includes grant/reshare/billing/code/console unless the client ticks it; annual reconfirmation instead of a hard expiry; revocation is immediate for not-yet-dispatched steps (already-dispatched panel/registrar operations cannot be recalled — stated residual limit).

**Verification & identifiers:** effective risk = max(declared, catalogue) with an explicit, tested `LOWERED_RISK` list. A destructive person-initiated action needs a fresh `DestructivePreview` fingerprint (switch `confirm.required`, default-off then deprecation-noticed on). Idempotency keys are namespaced `org:service:action:actor:<key>` with a request hash stored in `operation.desired` (409 on mismatch). The operation persists its authorization basis (mode, family, token id, support-grant id, delegation id, approval ids). Audit keeps existing resource/hash fields; written in-transaction once lock contention is measured; retention per row class; PII moves to an erasable side table for new rows (existing chain history is out of scope for re-chaining).

## 7. What cannot be undone, and what replaces undo

| Action | Why it cannot be undone | Replacement |
|---|---|---|
| Domain transfer-out / registrant change / DS publish | External registry action, not reversible by us. | Preview + explicit typed confirmation + ≥HIGH risk; classified per TLD/registrar (redemption exists for some, not others — never assumed). |
| Sent mail / notifications | Cannot be recalled once delivered. | Preview states "this will be sent"; no send is silent. |
| Payments, refunds, partner payouts | Money movement is external and mostly final. | CRITICAL risk (second person or time lock), row-locked computation, cooling-off on destination changes, audit trail. |
| Panel password reset (Pterodactyl `panel.password`) | Overwrites the only credential; old one is gone. | Ownership re-verified before the reset; the person who reset it is recorded; rotation is offered, not reversed. |
| `gamedb.delete` / `gamedb.rotate` | Wings backups are full-server, slot-limited — no per-database undo exists in the panel. | Class D: preview + confirm + ≥HIGH; capped content pre-copy to platform storage where feasible, never a promise of a clean database restore. |
| `mailbox.restore` (restoring an older mailbox state) | May overwrite mail received since the backup. | Preview names the backup date and warns "mail received since may be overwritten"; never silent. |
| Pterodactyl panel-direct actions (bypassing the platform) | Outside platform control entirely. | Labelled class D and discouraged; platform-issued sub-user actions are the supported, undoable path. |
| Restoring an account/environment where a person's access was later **revoked** | Undo must never resurrect access someone lost for a security reason. | `UndoEligibility` refuses to undo security/abuse/revocation/system actions, and a revocation-epoch check blocks restoring a copy older than the latest revocation until artefact cleanup re-runs. |
| The DB portion of a code/content restore (`wp.update`, `import.run`, `database.import`) | A files-only pre-copy does not capture database writes made after it. | Explicit "N minutes of writes since <time> will be lost" preview, or an opt-in side-by-side `restore.test` instead of an in-place overwrite. |
| Zone/resolver-observed effects (cached TTLs, ACME validations already completed, mail already routed elsewhere) | DNS *data* can be restored; effects already observed by third parties cannot be recalled. | `zone.restore` recreates the zone content from its version history; the preview is worded as restoring data, not undoing effects. |
