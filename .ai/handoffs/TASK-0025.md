**Outcome per package.**

- **pay-and-restore (owner decision 23).** With the default-off rule `services.reinstate` on, a cancelled service inside
  its restore window comes back after payment through the ordinary resume: a paid invoice of a TERMINATED dunning case
  restores it free while the paid period lasts; otherwise `POST /v1/services/{id}/reinstate` charges one new period from
  the credit (`billing.wallet.spend`), and a short credit records the wish, which the next covering top-up fulfils with
  exactly one charge. An undone cancellation is billed again; the purge spares a paid service and its carried sites;
  money lifts only the `payment` hold. Unconditionally (not behind the rule): a chargeback-cancelled service cannot be
  resumed by the customer (409 `chargeback_cancelled`). Read-only `onhost:billing:reinstatement-audit` lists services
  that would be affected; `--apply` needs one `--service`.
- **withdrawal (owner decision 17).** With the default-off rule `billing.withdrawal` on, a consumer (class at order time)
  withdraws within 14 days of the order in the panel (`service.delete`, HIGH, fresh step-up) or finance records a letter
  by its sent date (`billing.refund.execute`, CRITICAL, four eyes). Order: suspend (hold `withdrawal`) → credit note of
  the paid, unused part of the lines to the credit (`returnToCredit`, express agreement recorded as consent) → cancel
  through the terminate saga (final backup). The customer cannot resume it. B2B orders and registered domains are
  refused (notice in the cart quote and in the texts); one withdrawal per order line; a paid order with nothing
  delivered is cancelled whole. `onhost:withdrawals:finish` (hourly :25) completes refused steps.

- MEDIUM the resume 402 carries the full quote (credit, shortfall, invoices) only for `billing.wallet.read`; otherwise

**Review round 3 (billing) — resolved.** Full list with `file:line` in `.ai/tasks/TASK-0025.md` → *Findings*.

- MEDIUM auto-renew is never switched on without the customer: a restore (paid reinstate or undone cancellation) takes
  auto-renew from the record of the cancellation being taken back only (`deletion`, or `deletion_cancelled` once undone;
  same subscription id; `cancel_at_period_end` counts as off); no record means OFF, never the organization's
  `auto_renew_default`. `expire()` writes `auto_renew=false` on the row it ends. A customer who switched auto-renew off or
  asked to end with the period and then pays to restore gets exactly the paid period.
- MEDIUM the withdrawal notice states only what really reaches the credit: `estimate()` splits `to_credit` /
  `off_documents` per document like `InvoiceService::giveBack`; `withdrawal.accepted` carries both (`estimate` true for a
  service, false for a whole order, `already_returned`); an order withdrawal publishes it only after the cancellation
  with what moved (an already-settled FAILED order: "na kredit se nic nevrací, vše už bylo vráceno dříve"); the mandatory
  mail `withdrawal-accepted` names both parts in `{{postup}}` (vars `castka` → `postup`, template version bumps on seed);
  `withdrawal.refunded` titles the credited part only; the panel seam's confirm text and estimate show `to_credit`.

**security-boundaries.md bullets:**

- Spending account credit to restore a service needs `billing.wallet.spend` at organization scope (not org_admin, not a
  service guest, not API tokens); money lifts only the `payment` hold. With `services.reinstate` on, taking a
  cancellation back (it bills again) needs the same permission, and the payment-required answer shows the organization's
  credit and invoices only to `billing.wallet.read`. A service the consumer withdrew from never gets a chargeback.

  service guest does not see the refund, the paid lines or the order). An order held for the staff risk review cannot be
  withdrawn by the customer (the review stays the staff's).
