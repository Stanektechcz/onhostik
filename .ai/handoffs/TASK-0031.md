## Outcome

A VAT number a customer gave is checked in the EU register (VIES) and the answer is kept as evidence; reverse charge
applies only to a business of another EU state whose number VIES confirmed at most 30 days before the quote or issue
(or under a staff override), otherwise today's destination VAT with a `vat_review` flag. Off until
`ONHOST_VIES_ENABLED=true`.

## Release notes for existing customers

- Nothing changes for existing customers' money at deploy: VIES is off (`ONHOST_VIES_ENABLED=false`), the migration writes no existing row,
  `tax.vies_recheck` is off by default, and `onhost:vat:verify` is a dry run unless `--apply`.
- Legacy rows are read as before: a stored `valid` without a source keeps reverse charge but is flagged `vat_review`
  (`legacy_unverified`); a stored `payer` (written only by the old dev seeder) keeps self-billing VAT.
- Once the operator switches VIES on: a newly given or changed VAT number is checked; a business of another EU state whose number is not
  VIES-valid (or whose check is older than 30 days) is charged its country's VAT with `vat_review` until verified; an invalid number is told
  to the billing contacts of a customer in another EU state (mail + portal note); a Czech organization gets an info note in the portal only.
- Existing partners: `onhost:vat:verify` lists partners (Czech ones included) whose number was never checked (group `partner`); until
  `--apply` their self-billing documents stay 0 % and say "Registrace dodavatele k DPH neověřena." (flag `vat_review`).
- Issued documents are never changed; `onhost:vat:verify --csv` lists past VAT invoices to EU B2B customers who gave a VAT ID for the
  accountant.
- Partner self-billing: every real partner document was 0 % before (nothing wrote `payer`); after the partner's number is VIES-valid, a CZ
  partner is billed at `standard_rates.CZ`, another EU state under reverse charge.
- Review round 3: the number must be of the partner's own country and VIES must register it to the partner's name; otherwise the document
  stays 0 % ("Registrace dodavatele k DPH neověřena.", flagged for finance) and no VAT is paid out. Finance accepts a genuine name difference
  with the staff override (step-up + four eyes). The partner portal shows the document's VAT and its note, not finance's review flag.
  Payouts already requested or paid keep their document and are paid what it says.
