## Concerns

1. `CartCapacity::fits` via `QuoteService` still asks without an organization (read-only "fits?"): the cart may say "fits" and
   the paid order then waits in `ScheduleNodeStep` with the tenancy reason, retried every 15 min, `capacity.unavailable` event to
   operators. Follow-up: pass `organization` from `QuoteService` (its path, not this task's).
2. With (a), single-customer open aaPanel nodes stop taking new customers — by order or by staff move: new shared-web sales and
   evacuations go to ISPConfig, to an empty node, or to a node the operator closes (program D11). Operators should know before
   release; an evacuation of a closed shared aaPanel node needs a closed (or per-organization empty) target.
3. A web move refused at the target step fails at once (named node) or after the retry budget (picked); `ServiceMigrationService`
   does not pre-check at start, so `service.evacuate` lists such moves as started and they fail on the operations board with the
   node and the tenancy command in the error.
