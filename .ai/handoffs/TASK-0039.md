## Outcome

- **Staff reach, shadow first (IF-4).** `Authorizer::basis()` tells a member's binding (`member`) from the platform's reach
  (`staff`: a global binding or a JIT elevation). Staff reach on a CUSTOMER-audience permission is still allowed while
  `ONHOST_STAFF_REACH_ENFORCED=false` (default) and written once per person, key, organization and day to `security_events`
  kind `authz.staff_reach` (roles, scope, route). With the switch on it is refused (also in `permissionsAt`). Staff-audience keys
  are untouched. `operator:authz:staff-reach --days=7` reads the log and says when enforcing is due.
- **Staff reach is CRITICAL where the catalogue says so.** A customer CRITICAL key (backup.delete, organization.close) reached by
  staff reach is CRITICAL — a second person, or the sole approver's time lock; a member keeps the HIGH floor. The RiskFloorTest pin
  of TASK-0037 is now the proof.
- **StaffActor + staff mode (IF-8).** `CommandContext::$staffMode` is set by `ApiContext::context()` for `/v1/staff/*` only (never
  for a token). `StaffActor::acts()` = staff mode + a person acting for themselves + an active staff account. It replaces every
  `is_staff` shortcut: the customer parameter filter, hold lifting, maintenance pass, who makes a hold, the reinstatement credit
  gate / "staff always" (the recorded request keeps `staff:<id>`), and the console pre-flight (now: the issuer recorded in the
  descriptor, or a current member). A member of staff on a customer route is the customer they act as. The staff route aliases
  `POST /v1/staff/services/{id}/actions` and `/reinstate` give staff the same controllers in staff mode. An architecture test
  allow-lists the remaining `is_staff` reads (code only).
- **Forced purge (IF-9).** `purge` with `force`, or any `archive_before_delete` that is not a definite yes, is
  `staff.service.delete` declared CRITICAL (`ServiceActionCommand::skipsCustomerProtection`). A forced purge never skips the final
  archive (`final_archive_required` 422). A customer who sends the flags is refused (403) instead of having them dropped.
- **Token principal view (IF-5).** A token sees only its own organization's bindings — never a global binding or an elevation
  (Authorizer, and the bus: `IdentityCommandAuthorizer` re-attaches the token from `token:<id>`). `X-Organization` / `?organization=`
  naming another organization → 403 `token_organization_mismatch` (TokenRouteScope, before any controller); no header → the
  token's organization. Tokens bound to none keep their person's organization bindings until `ONHOST_TOKEN_ORGANIZATION_REQUIRED=true`
  (then 403 `token_unbound`); `operator:tokens:unbound --dry-run` lists them and tokens used on another organization (90 days).
- **Staff SSO (IF-16).** `PanelLoginCommand` through the bus (HIGH + step-up): an open ticket about THAT service opened by a current
  member in the portal/API, a reason ≥ 10, `staff.console` of the service's family (`StaffActor::CONSOLE_FAMILIES`), and without
  consent (`ticket.meta.support_access`, level console, by a member holding the service's console) a second person / the solo time
  lock. Eligibility is checked before an approval is opened. The link is parked 60 s and handed out once — never in the replay
  store, audit or outbox. Event `service.staff_panel_login` → customer in-app (security, warn) + mandatory mail `staff-panel-login`
  to the owner; staff security line with the reason.

- **Review round 1 (1 HIGH, 2 MEDIUM, all fixed).** (1) Staff mode now survives the queue: `OperationService::start` writes
  `desired.staff_mode` from the starting context only (a caller's key is dropped) and `OperationRunner::context` rebuilds
  `staffMode` from it, so an abuse.action quarantine settles as an ABUSE hold and a staff suspend as REVIEW again (they had
  settled as the customer's pause); the admin drawer's assistant sends staff-confirmed actions to `/v1/staff/services/{id}/actions`.
  (2) A ticket opened, or a consent given, by a staff account never counts for the panel sign-on. (3) The panel-login handler
  refuses when the consent is gone and the bus consumed no second person / waiver for the run.
- **Review round 2 (1 HIGH, 2 MEDIUM, all fixed).** (1) The one-time panel link was kept verbatim for 24 h by the HTTP
  idempotency middleware when the request carried an `Idempotency-Key`, and replayed without the bus (no audit, no customer
  notice): the staff POST panel-login route opts out of `idempotency`; a same-key repeat now reaches the bus → 409
  `panel_login_spent`; the answer is `Cache-Control: no-store`. (2) The shadow row is written after the caller's transaction ends
  (commit or rollback, `afterCommit` + `afterRollBack`), the day's cache mark only by a written row — a refusal that rolled back
  the bus no longer erased the row while the day stayed marked, and a failing insert can no longer abort a PostgreSQL transaction.
  (3) A queued run started with an API token keeps the token: `desired.token_id` from the starting context only, the runner
  re-attaches it before each privileged step (`IdentityCommandAuthorizer::asToken`, now public) and stops with `access_revoked`
  once it is revoked or expired; the run actor carries `token:<id>` so chained runs keep it.
- **Review round 3 (1 HIGH, fixed).** P0-09 was open outside /v1: `auth:sanctum` on the web routes takes a bearer token, and
  `/surfaces/onhost-panel.js?organization=B` served B's panel data to the token of A (without the parameter: the person's first
  membership); the console pre-flight and the staff pages answered tokens too. All nine web `auth:sanctum` routes now also take
  `token.scope`, which refuses any token outside /v1 (session only); a route-table test pins it for future routes.

## Release notes

- Staff: service actions that need staff powers (lifting an abuse/review/payment hold, staff resizes, archive skip, forced flags)
  and restores on a customer's behalf go through `/v1/staff/services/{id}/actions` and `/v1/staff/services/{id}/reinstate`; on the
  customer routes a member of staff is treated as the customer. The early purge (`/v1/staff/provisioning/services/{id}/purge`) now
  takes a second person (or, for the sole approver, the 24 h time lock) and always the final archive.
- Staff SSO into a customer panel: `POST /v1/staff/services/{id}/panel-login` with `ticket_id` + `reason`; without the customer's
  consent on the ticket a second approver. The customer is notified immediately (in-app + owner mail).
- API tokens: act for their own organization only; another organization in `X-Organization` is refused; no header = the token's
  organization. Before the release: run `operator:tokens:unbound --dry-run` and notify the listed owners (unbound tokens and tokens
  used across organizations).
- Operators: watch `operator:authz:staff-reach --days=7` daily; when it has been empty for 7 days (after P0-15 gave the listed
  workflows staff keys), set `ONHOST_STAFF_REACH_ENFORCED=true`. Switch `ONHOST_TOKEN_ORGANIZATION_REQUIRED=true` after the notice.
- Existing customers: nothing changes for their own use (no switch flipped); only cross-organization use of a token stops.

## Concerns

- Admin console flows on customer routes keep working only while the switch is off (by design); the shadow log is the list.
- Consent is read by the bus and again by the handler: withdrawn in between → the handler refuses without a consumed approval
  (fixed in review round 1). Granted after an approval was obtained → the repeat runs as consented, the approval stays unspent
  and expires (accepted, tested).
- Deploy: a staff `suspend` queued by the old code and settled after the deploy has no `desired.staff_mode` and would settle as
  the customer's pause — deploy with no staff suspend runs in flight (they finish in seconds).
- Deploy: runs queued with a token before the deploy carry no `desired.token_id` and finish on the person's view, as before.
- A retry (`OperationService::retry`) keeps the original actor and `desired`, so retrying a token-started run whose token is gone
  stops at its first step — the same as a run whose permission was revoked (H315); staff start a new action instead.
