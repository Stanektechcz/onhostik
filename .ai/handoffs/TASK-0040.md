## Outcome

- **One balance, one payout (P1).** A payout request locks the partner row, reads the payable commissions under that
  lock, checks the amount against them and allocates in the same transaction; the payout's amount is the sum it
  allocated (a mismatch throws and rolls back). Paying checks the invariant again (`payout_allocation_mismatch`).
- **The IBAN comes from the payout account only (P2).** New `partner_payout_accounts`: set by the partner organization's
  owner alone (`partner.payout_account.manage`, HIGH, owner-only, never staff), with a mandatory `legal-notice` mail to
  the owner and the billing e-mail, usable after 7 days (`PayoutAccounts::COOLING_DAYS`), cancellable while it cools off,
  ISO 13616 check digits verified. An IBAN a payout was paid to **before the cut-over** (the moment migration `000890` ran,
  one `system_settings` row) is the confirmed account without a row (grandfathered, read-time — no customer data written);
  nothing paid later becomes an account (review round 1). The payout request's `iban` is optional and must equal the account (409
  `payout_account_mismatch`; 409 `payout_account_missing` without one); it never writes anything. Automatic payouts follow
  the same rule (`partners.iban` is no longer trusted).
- **Paid once, from `approved`, by somebody else (P8, TD-4).** `payout.pay` is CRITICAL: a second person signs the payment
  (the sole operator: the TASK-0037 time lock). The domain refuses the payout's requester and its approver as payer
  (waived only on the sole operator's time-locked payment), pays only `approved` (was: also `requested`), under a row
  lock (a second pay gets `payout_not_approved`). Review round 1: the payment checks the destination again — a bank
  transfer goes only to an IBAN that was the confirmed account when it was asked for (a payout naming its account row: that
  row's IBAN), else 409 `payout_account_unconfirmed`; a leftover of the old code passes only after finance released it with a
  recorded reason (hold, confirm with the partner, release). Approve refuses a frozen payout and the requester; the payout request
  itself takes a step-up (HIGH).
- **Existing payouts: dry run first, freeze only the anomalous.** `onhost:partners:payout-anomalies` lists open payouts
  above their allocation and bank transfers to an IBAN that was not confirmed when requested, and prints a list digest;
  `--apply --digest=<it>` freezes exactly that list via the bus and nothing when a new scan differs (review round 1). A partner's first payout is listed, never frozen; a payout finance released is not frozen again.
  Frozen payouts cannot be approved or paid; finance rejects them (commissions back) or releases them with a reason
  (`POST /v1/staff/partners/payouts/{id}/unfreeze|freeze`).
- **Portal reads need `partner.portal.read` (P5)** — owner, org_admin, billing_admin, partner role (was every member via
  `organization.read`). **Client contacts and dunning are masked** in the partner view (`contact` null, `contact_masked`,
  overdue reads `ok`); staff keep them. `onhost:partners:masking-notice --send` tells each active partner once (in-app +
  `legal-notice` mail), per program §10 O9.
