## Outcome

1. **`GET /v1/me` over a token (P0-09, PA-04; the P0-16 re-check MEDIUM).** `AuthController::organizationsOf`: the portal session
   (the person) keeps every membership in full; a token bound to an organization gets that membership only; a token bound to
   none keeps the list — the organization acted for (header, or the person's first membership) in full, every other one as
   `{id, name}` only. **Choice recorded:** an unbound token still acts for every organization of its person until
   `ONHOST_TOKEN_ORGANIZATION_REQUIRED=true`, so it keeps the names to choose one with `X-Organization`, never their billing
   identity, settings or role. `data.organization` is unchanged (it is already the token's own for a bound token).
2. **`archive.restore` through a global staff binding (P0-08, IF-4).** `ServiceArchiveService::assertMayRestore` no longer asks
   `Authorizer::can` (where a global binding or a JIT elevation covers every organization). A person is asked on **customer
   bindings only** (`Authorizer::customerPermissionsAt`: organization, project, resource bindings), on the **token's view** when a
   token sent the command (`IdentityCommandAuthorizer::asToken`, also closes the row-133 LOW "reads the source on the person").
   **Staff mode** (`/v1/staff/services/{id}/actions`, `StaffActor::acts`) asks `StaffActor::may` for `staff.backup.read` (404
   otherwise) and a staff role carrying `backup.restore` (403 `forbidden`, `permission: backup.restore`, `scope: staff`). A member
   of staff who is a member of the organization restores there on the customer route as that member.
3. **The three secondary gates (audit §4).** Already fixed by TASK-0029 (`22dae65`, D29.7): `ServiceSpecService::apply` asks the
   bus per step (`CommandAuthorizer::authorize` on a `ServiceActionCommand`) plus the token scope; `ActionHookService::create`,
   `::trigger`, `::orphanReason` and `DiscordService::execute` ask `ServiceActionCommand::permissionFor` and refuse step-up
   actions. Verified by `ServiceActionSecondaryGatesTest` (green) and pinned by a source guard in `LastPhaseZeroLeaksTest` (no
   `'service.manage'` literal in the three files). No code change.

## Release notes (who loses what)

- **API integrations reading `GET /v1/me` with a token.** Bound token: `data.organizations` now holds only the token's
  organization (other organizations of the same person disappear). Unbound token: the other organizations shrink to
  `{id, name}` (no `billing`, `settings`, `role`, `slug`, `customer_class`…). A client that read another organization's billing
  identity through a token loses it — by design; the portal is unchanged.
- **Staff with a global backup role (backup_dr_admin, platform_owner) on the customer routes.** `POST /v1/services/archives/{id}/restore`
  and `POST /v1/services/{id}/actions` `archive.restore` with `X-Organization` of a customer they are not a member of: now 404
  (was a started restore, shadow-logged). Use `POST /v1/staff/services/{id}/actions` with `archive.restore` (staff mode: step-up,
  the bus's usual rules, a second person in an organization of one's own).
- **A JIT elevation into an organization** no longer counts for an archive restore on the customer route (an elevation is the
  platform's reach, as `customerPermissionsAt` defines it everywhere else).
- **A member of staff who is also a member of the customer's organization, on the staff route:** an archive restore there now
  needs the staff keys (`staff.backup.read` + a staff role with `backup.restore`), not the membership; on the customer route they
  keep restoring as the member they are.
- Customers: nothing changes (owners, org admins, project-scoped operators restore as before; `ArchiveRestoreScopeTest` green).
