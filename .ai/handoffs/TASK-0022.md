## Concerns

1. OWNER: a solo operator must set `ONHOST_FOUR_EYES=false` before deploy, or the price list freezes (every price change waits
   for a second person).
2. OWNER: `onhost:catalog:revise --apply` and `onhost:catalog:state active` run as the system actor without a second person
   (gate = shell access + code review of `CatalogRevisions`).
3. OWNER: the four-eyes boundary is `CatalogCommand` only; loyalty levels, registrar costs, chargeback settings, partner
   tiers are not four-eyes.
4. OWNER: `ONHOST_ADDON_RENEWALS` — ending an ipv4/CDN/mail add-on only lowers the entitlement (the panel/edge is not
   changed); decide before switching on.
5. Residual: staff `resize` `params.limits` / `service.create` `config.limits` (cpu_pct …) and boolean upgrades are not
   guarded; a second raise delivered during a running resize is `panel: pending` until `onhost:limit-raise push --apply`.
6. Rebase: `RoleCatalog.php` — `billing.limit_raise.waive` is appended to the existing `billing_finance_admin` line (no
   block seam in that array). Conflicts only if another task edits the same line; resolve by keeping both additions.
7. Resolved in review round 1: a revision carries a promo price into the new version (`keep_promos`); a staff publish in the
   console still ends a promo (a campaign of its version), as before.
9. OWNER / money: a prepaid raise that ends early is not credited for the unused days — its parent terminated (the add-ons
   are cancelled with it) or the customer ending it at once. A customer's end could instead map to cancel-at-period-end.
10. Advisory guard: `LimitRaises::assertCanEnd` stops a customer's terminate of a raise the service uses, but a raise whose
   subscription the customer cancels expires at its period end as the system actor and may leave the service above its limit
   (a lower panel limit only blocks new resources; nothing is deleted).
11. Before `ONHOST_LIMIT_RAISE_CUSTOMER_ORDERS=true`: `LimitRaiseLine` checks the parent against the organization only; a
   project-scoped member must be checked against the parent's project (as other add-on paths do).
8. Pre-existing, out of scope: `DomainService::assertActionAllowed` trusts caller-offered `approvalIds`; add-on cart lines with
   `parent_service_id` are not checked against the parent's `addon_products`.

## Release notes for existing customers

- Nothing changes for any existing service, subscription, plan version, price or add-on. New plan versions appear only when the
  operator runs `php artisan onhost:catalog:revise --apply`; customers on the old version keep it and its price.
- New managed databases no longer show point-in-time recovery; existing ones keep their flag.
- Public pages: the database page no longer promises PITR or a connection count; e-shop product counts are a recommendation;
  the student/school programme page is withdrawn.
- A limit of a running service can be raised for a fee (staff-assisted first; self-service only once enabled). An unpaid
  raise stays through the dunning suspension stage (paying then keeps it) and ends at the termination stage; a raise paid after
  its service was cancelled is not delivered and the money returns to credit; a raise given free renews, if switched back on,
  at the list price; an add-on bought before is never ended or newly billed by this change.
- Staff: price and plan changes now need a second person (unless `ONHOST_FOUR_EYES=false`); switching an automation rule
  needs a step-up; a raw resize above what the service holds is refused — use a limit raise.
