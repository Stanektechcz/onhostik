## Outcome

Root cause: the overdue and dunning jobs compared UTC instants and counted UTC days, while every document prints its due
date as the seller's (Prague) accounting day. So from Prague midnight until the job ran, an invoice was overdue on its own
printed due date, and dunning ran a day early. The two night-failing test files were test artefacts. WithdrawalTest built its
expected dates from the UTC day; the product was right. LimitRaiseTest had a flat one-day travel before the 06:00 dunning
recheck. e104507 already fixed that in the base (it was not in f55a6d8, the revision the release record gated).

## Release notes (customer impact)

- **Overdue notices on the due date itself stop.** An invoice due between midnight and about 03:15 Prague time was marked
  overdue at 01:15 UTC on the date printed as its due date. The customer was mailed "Doklad … je po splatnosti" with the
  whole day still left to pay. This hit postpaid renewal invoices issued by the 22:20, 23:20 and 00:20 UTC runs (summer; 23:20
  and 00:20 in winter) and any invoice or proforma issued at night. Now an invoice turns overdue on the first daily run
  after its printed due date.
- **Dunning follows the printed calendar.** For cases due in the last two UTC hours of a day (the last hour in winter),
  reminders, grace, the suspension or billing limit and the termination schedule came one day early. They now come on the
  printed day. No amount changes.
- **Existing records:** no issued document, no OVERDUE mark, no reminder and no stored termination date is rewritten
  (append-only). An open case due in that window is recounted from the next tick and may wait one day for its next step. It
  never goes back a state. Reminders are not repeated.
- **Domain expiry days follow the printed date.** The panel and API ("expiruje za N dní", `days_to_expiry`, the renewal
  bucket) said one day more than the calendar from midnight to 01:00/02:00 Prague. At that hour a domain seven days before
  its expiry got the 14-day notice ("expiruje za 14 dní"), and the 7-day one followed at 02:00. Now the count goes from the
  Prague day to the expiry date, and the notice for that day comes from Prague midnight. Renewals, retries and when a domain
  counts as expired are unchanged. The "already expired" wording of a failed renewal payment is tied to the registry
  expiry itself.
- Withdrawal deadlines, withdrawal refunds and renewals were already right. Their tests failed at night because of how the
  tests were written.
