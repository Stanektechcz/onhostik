## Outcome

`domains/Organizations/GrantPolicy.php` is the one place deciding who may invite, change, remove, transfer to, or give/take a
project role for whom (program I1, I2, I3, I4, I8, I9, I11; unknown role keys fail closed). Every membership and
project-membership op of `OrganizationsCommandHandler` goes through it; the service keeps the data invariants for every caller.

## Release notes for existing customers

- A member can no longer be invited again (409); change their role on the team page instead. Old pending links that would give a
  current member less are accepted without changing anything.
- An organization admin can no longer change or remove a member whose role holds something the admin's role does not — in
  practice the **billing admin** (credit spending is withheld from org admins, owner decision 20). The owner can. This is the one
  refusal existing teams may notice.
- Adding or removing a project role now asks for the same fresh confirmation (step-up) as inviting a member, and project roles are
  limited to developer, cloud/game operator, mail/DNS/domain manager, security auditor, support contact and viewer. Existing project
  roles outside that list keep working; nothing existing is changed.
- Retried service actions: the same `Idempotency-Key` from another organization, another person or for another service is now its
  own request; the same key with a different body gets 409. A retry spanning the deploy may get a new operation id (a second run is
  still stopped by `operation_in_progress`). Two identical requests sent at the same moment: one runs, the other gets 409 (was a 500).
