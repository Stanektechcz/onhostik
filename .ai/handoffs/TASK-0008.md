**Outcome.** A customer's second web hosting on ISPConfig is no longer refused by the panel after they paid for it.
The client account's limits are what the **organization** holds on that panel, not what one service happens to sell.

**Files.**

- `domains/Services/Web/ClientAllowance.php` (new) — the organization's totals on one provider instance: sums
  `sites`, `nvme_gb`, `databases`, `mailboxes`, `cron_concurrency`, `domains`, `ftp_accounts`, ORs `ssh`, and leaves
  the per-site numbers (`php_workers`, `quota_gb_per_mailbox`, …) as the service's own. Carried sites are **not**
  counted (their parent's plan already covers them) and everything but TERMINATED is.
- `providers/IspConfig/IspConfigWebProvider.php` — `ensureClient()` reads `client_entitlements` when the spec carries
  it (falling back to `entitlements`), and on the **reuse** path brings the existing client's limits up to it through
  the existing `updateClientLimits()`, which calls the panel only when something really differs.
- `domains/Provisioning/Workflows/ProvisionWebsiteWorkflow.php` — the provisioning spec carries
  `client_entitlements => ClientAllowance::of($service)`.
- `tests/Contract/IspConfigContractTest.php` (3 new cases), `tests/Contract/ClientAllowanceTest.php` (new).

**Contracts.** One new optional spec key, `client_entitlements`. No endpoint, event, schema or config key changes; a
spec without the key behaves exactly as before (covered by a test).
