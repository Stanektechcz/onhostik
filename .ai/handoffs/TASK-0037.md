## Outcome

- **Risk floor (IF-13).** The bus runs every operation at `PermissionCatalog::effectiveRisk()` = max(declared, floor of its
  permission). The floor is the catalogue risk, except a customer permission rated CRITICAL floors at HIGH (customers have no
  second person — D8 rejected the staff queue for them; S4-03 brings the customer decider). `LOWERED_RISK` is empty and pinned.
  61 operations newly take a fresh step-up (3 customer, 58 staff; list below). The old special case "CRITICAL staff permission
  forces CRITICAL" is subsumed. AI actors are judged on the effective risk too.
- **Waiver scoped + time lock (IF-10).** `ONHOST_FOUR_EYES=false` spares only the sole active holder of `iam.approval.decide`;
  everybody else gets an ordinary approval request (which the sole approver decides). The sole approver's own critical action
  (and any `requiresApproval()` op — price changes) is refused on the first attempt and opens a time-locked request
  (`payload.time_lock.not_before` = now + 24 h, `ONHOST_FOUR_EYES_TIME_LOCK_HOURS`, min 1); the first repeat after that, with a
  fresh step-up, runs it (atomic spend, audited `waived:single-operator`); the requester may cancel it (own rejection →
  state `cancelled`), approving their own stays refused. Staff hear `iam.approval.time_locked`.
- **Staff read keys (IF-18).** `staff.support.ticket.read`, `staff.backup.read`, `staff.billing.read` (NORMAL, staff) added and
  granted next to the customer keys (support L1–L3 + manager; billing finance admin + operator; backup DR admin).
  `Staff/SupportController` (index, show) and `Staff/WithdrawalController` (index) ask the staff keys. Customer keys are NOT
  removed from staff roles (that is P0-15 after a shadow log).
- **RoleResolver + seeder (P0-11).** `RoleResolver::grantable()` (unknown → nothing) / `coveredForRevoke()` (unknown →
  everything) / `catalogue()`. `AuthorizationSeeder` runs in one transaction and applies only the difference (never deletes a
  whole role). An architecture allow-list stops new direct readers of `RoleCatalog::all()`.
- **Dry-run (IF-13).** `php artisan onhost:iam:risk-floor-report [--days=30]` — read-only: API tokens and service accounts
  that ran an operation under a HIGH+ permission without a step-up (they will be refused — tell owners first), and per action
  how many people in the portal/console will see the step-up dialog.

## Release notes for existing customers (and operators)

Customers — from this release these actions ask for a fresh confirmation of identity (step-up: authenticator code or
password dialog) in the portal, and are no longer possible with an API token:
1. **Publishing DNSSEC keys (DS record) to the registry** (`domain.publish_ds`, permission DNSSEC management).
2. **Changing a domain's contacts / registrant** (`domain.contact`, permission registrant change).
3. **Paying for and downloading the archive of a cancelled service** (`service.archive.download`; tokens were already
   refused since TASK-0030).
Nothing else changes for customers (the GDPR erasure and backup deletion keep their step-up, no second person).
Before release run `php artisan onhost:iam:risk-floor-report` and tell the owners of every token it lists.

Operators / staff — 58 staff operations under HIGH permissions now take the step-up the console asks for:
capacity (`capacity.decide`, `.budget`), catalogue panel navigation, abuse cases (triage, notify, action, close), cyber
incidents (open, transition, evidence), compliance timers (submit) and data-request processing, status-page incidents (open,
update, resolve, postmortem when public; security incidents), maintenance (schedule, cancel, complete), SLA credits
(candidates, approve, reject), loyalty campaigns/missions, marketplace curation and disputes, partners (approve, state,
payout approve/reject, tier recompute), provisioning under `provider.instance.manage` (instances discover, nodes, placements,
registrar costs/policy, game eggs/bootstrap/allocations/node/migrate/evacuate, service migrate/evacuate, rebalance),
`provisioning.freeze` (load, automation risk), drift resolution, tenant sandbox, registrar connections (disable, enable, staff
sync). One operator (`ONHOST_FOUR_EYES=false`): critical actions and price changes of the only approver wait 24 h (plan a
day ahead; cancel on the approvals page); anybody else with a critical permission now asks the approver.
