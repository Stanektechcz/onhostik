## Objective

Taking a cancellation back also brings back the sites the service carried: they no longer keep a `terminate_at` of
their own for the purge to collect thirty days later with the customer's files and databases.

## Context

Audit row 93 (`docs/runbooks/production-readiness-audit.md`), CURRENT_STATE bullet *Undoing a cancellation brings back
the sites the service carried*. `cancellationCleared` nulled `terminate_at` only on the service it was called on; the
carried sites had been ended with their own `terminate_at` and no `included.held_by`, so the resume step skipped them.
