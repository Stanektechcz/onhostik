## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The second person of an owner recovery is no party (approving, opening, continuing, completing).
- [x] A transfer recovery ends in an offer the heir accepts in person with a fresh step-up; a heir made a guest or gone is refused.
- [x] The recovered owner's cancel of a transfer goes to a staff review continued only by a second person with evidence; others

## Security considerations

New refusals only for illegitimate steps (a party approving, the account in doubt cancelling, a stale heir, an expired token, a
console for a person who lost it). The relay closes on three failed alive checks (fail closed). A kill marker lives 3 h in the
cache (longer than the relay's 2 h cap); a cache flush forgets it — the person's permission is still asked on every check.

## Migration considerations

additive; an old relay keeps working without the checks).
