## Objective

A cash refund never pays out more than the purchased credit that is still unspent; a cancellation that fails (at its final
archive or at the switch-off) leaves the service in the state the panel really has, and the operators see it.

## Context

Both found by TASK-0094 (PR #90, "Not fixed, reported"). `WalletService::refundableBalance` returned
min(available, purchased − refunded) although its docblock promised "− consumed": 1000 bought, 800 spent, 800 returned
offered 1000 as cash. A `terminate` failing at `finalArchiveStep` was compensated to SUSPENDED with `terminate_at` null
while the site still served.

- H2: purchased credit keyed on the ledger transaction kind (`topup`, or a reversal of a `refund`), never on

## Contracts affected

No endpoint, schema or event change. Behaviour: `refundableBalance()` is lower whenever spent purchased credit was
followed by returned/bonus/staff credit; a failed `terminate` settles ACTIVE or SUSPENDED by the panel's truth (event
`service.active` or `service.deactivated` instead of `service.suspended`); an already SUSPENDED/FAILED service is left
as it is. New doctor row `lifecycle / no cancellation left unfinished` (WARN, not blocking).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] 1000 bought, 800 spent, 800 returned → refundable 200; a refund of 300 is refused, 200 goes through.
- [x] Spend order explicit and documented: purchased credit first, then other credit, then debt (later credit pays it).
- [x] Refund idempotent by key; never above what is available (holds count); per currency.
- [x] A terminate failing at the final archive leaves an ACTIVE hosting ACTIVE (panel serves), no `terminate_at`, no
      subscription cancelled; `operation.failed` published; the doctor row lists it.
- [x] An already suspended hosting stays SUSPENDED and switched off on the panel; no second "suspended"/"failed" message.
- [x] Panel refuses the switch-off → ACTIVE; switch-off done and a later step fails → SUSPENDED (panel off).
