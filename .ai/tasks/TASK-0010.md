## Objective

A suspension that the panel only partly carried out is no longer reported as finished: what keeps running on a
stopped service is recorded and the operators hear about it, so an abuse quarantine that left the cron running
cannot pass unnoticed.

## Context

Audit row 97, the counterpart of row 96 (TASK-0009) on the way in. `SuspensionDepth` exists because a quarantined
site went on sending mail from its own cron. `pause()` switched cron, FTP, game schedules, Node apps and outgoing
mail off and remembered what it switched off — but a **refusal** (aaPanel answers `status:false`, which `normalize()`
maps to `VALIDATION`, not a timeout) was swallowed: `pauseExtrasStep` returned `StepResult::done`, the saga wrote
SUSPENDED, and `pause_errors` went into the operation context, which nothing surfaces.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A refused switch-off leaves the service SUSPENDED (the site stays stopped) and is recorded, not swallowed.
- [x] What the panel refused is **not** remembered as paused — no later resume may switch it "back" on.
- [x] The record lives in a tag of its own, so writing the state does not wipe it.
- [x] The operators are told, with the reason the service was suspended.
- [x] The record is dropped once the service runs again, including when the resume step skips its work.

## Security considerations

An abuse quarantine that leaves the cron running is the failure this class exists to prevent, so the finding is
security-relevant. Two deliberate decisions:

1. **The suspension is not failed.** Failing the step would return the service to ACTIVE — serving *and* running its
   cron. A stopped site with a stubborn cron job is strictly better than both up.
2. **The health check stays silent.** `ServiceHealthCheck::text()` is read by customers through the assistant
   (`AssistantService`) and the ticket drafter; telling a quarantined customer what still runs for them is an
   invitation. The operators get the event; the customer gets nothing.

## Migration considerations

None. No schema change, no backfill, no sweep — the rule that existing services and customers are not touched holds
(`tags.suspension_still_running` only appears on the next suspension).

## Rollback

Revert the merge commit. The tag is additive and read by nothing else; a leftover tag is inert.
