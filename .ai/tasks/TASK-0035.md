## Objective

A person who leaves an organization — or is moved to a smaller role — keeps no way into it that is not the panel: their
Discord link is revoked, their action hooks are switched off, and every Discord read asks their current membership. An archive
of a cancelled service goes back only for somebody who may read its source and restore for the whole project or organization,
over a copy of the live target. The team page never shows admin controls to a person whose role is unknown.

## Decisions applied

- D3 / ruling #2: a Discord link and a hook are narrowed credentials of one person in one organization — the link needs a
  current membership and `organization.manage` (what creating it asks), a hook needs a current membership and its action's
  permission. Removal revokes all; a role change revokes what the new role could not have made.
- §2.11 / §10 defaults: the listener is a pure security fix going forward (applies directly); the leftovers of the past go
  through `operator:integrations:orphan-links`, dry run by default. Discord reads for pre-existing links of demoted members are
  scoped at once by `AssistantScope` (shows only what the person may see — illegitimate reach, no default_off switch).
  A non-member's link is revoked at its next use (illegitimate by definition).
- IF-11 / ruling #10: source = `backup.read` at the archived service's resource scope (org/project/resource bindings count);
  target = `backup.restore` at the target's project (or organization) scope — resource bindings (`svc_restore`) do not count.
  Enforced in `ServiceArchiveService::restore`, which both endpoints now reach. Removing the key from the `svc_restore` preset
  itself is TASK-0037's (catalogue owner); until then the permission stays in the role and the handler refuses the use.
- D9 pre-copy: `archive.restore` gets `safetyCopyStep('pre_restore')` in front (kind shared with `restore`, pruned at the
  DeletionPolicy retention). Fail closed: a safety copy row that FAILED is no longer promoted to `completed` on the retry
  (found while proving it — it affected `restore`, `database.import`, `reinstall` of web services too).
- Hooks are switched off, never deleted (the owner sees which one stopped and why); a re-admitted person's old hook stays off.

## Review round 1 (2026-09-27) — findings and resolutions

1. **security MEDIUM — cloud/data safety copy still promoted a "running" row** (`ServiceActionWorkflow::safetyCopyStep`).
   CONFIRMED, with one correction to the path. The automatic retry after an `AsyncStatus::UNKNOWN` poll does NOT call `run()`:
   `OperationRunner` keeps `external_handle` on a retryable failure, so the next tick polls again. `run()` is re-entered only
   by `OperationService::retry` after FAILED (it resets `step`, `context` and `external_handle`). That path is real: the poll
   FAILED (or stayed UNKNOWN until the 6 h budget, or our deadline passed and the operator acknowledged it), the retry found the
   row still `running`, called it `completed` and ran `rollback_snapshot` / `reinstall` / `restore` with no copy.
   - Red (HEAD `acd1040` workflow, new test `rolls a server back only over a snapshot the hypervisor confirmed, also on the
     operator retry`): `Failed asserting that two arrays are identical. -'snapshot' -'rollback' +'rollback'` — the retry rolled
     the VPS back with no second snapshot, over an unconfirmed one.
   - Fix: the snapshot row keeps its task handle (`meta.task`). A `running` row found by `run()` is confirmed only by the
     hypervisor's own answer on THAT task: SUCCEEDED → the copy (completed); RUNNING → wait on it; FAILED / UNKNOWN / no
     task on record (rows from before this release) → the row is marked `failed` and a NEW snapshot is taken; a hypervisor
     that does not answer → retryable step failure, nothing written. The lookup of "this operation's copy" ignores failed rows
     (so `afterAsyncSuccess` never completes the old one). Chosen over the reviewer's "list snapshots" option: a Proxmox
     snapshot half-made (`snapstate: prepare`) is listed too, so presence in the list is no proof.
   - Green: the test passes (`['snapshot', 'snapshot', 'rollback']`, one failed + one completed `pre_rollback` row, the
     completed one on the second task); `SafetyCopyTest` green.
2. **qa HIGH — no test isolates the target (resource-scope) check of `assertMayRestore`.** CONFIRMED as a coverage gap (the
   reviewer's reading is right: in `never lets a restore capability…` the 404 is the SOURCE check, and the archives endpoint's
   403 comes from the bus, which checks `ServiceArchiveCommand` at organization scope before `restore()` runs).
   - New test `refuses a restore held only through a share of the target, even to a person who may read the source`: an
     organization `viewer` (backup.read everywhere) with `svc_restore` on two targets — one without a project, one in a
     project — gets 403 `forbidden` with `permission: backup.restore` and `scope: organization` / `project`; no operation,
     no waive.
   - Red by mutation (target scope changed to `CommandScope::resource(...)`, i.e. the regression the reviewer named):
     `Expected response status code [403] but received 202.` At base `3b2a9fb` the same request is 202 as well (the generic
     endpoint had no source/target check at all). Restored; green.
3. **qa MEDIUM — the listener's idempotency under at-least-once delivery was claimed, not tested.** CONFIRMED as a gap, no
   bug: `orphans()` returns only linked/pending links and enabled hooks, and `revoke()`/`disable()` short-circuit too.
   - New test `does nothing more when the same removal is delivered again, even after the person was let back in`: the same
     `organization.member.removed` outbox message handled a second time (plus another relay) leaves exactly one
     `integration.discord.revoke` and one `integration.hook.disable` audit row; a late copy that arrives after the person was
     re-admitted as `org_admin` does not revoke the NEW link they made since (the listener asks today's membership).
   - Green on first run (regression pin; no red because there was no defect).
