## Objective

Phase T, T3 = metering M2 (`docs/proposals/metering-after-launch.md` §2): the monthly transfer a VPS/VDS plan sells (`traffic_tb`) is
measured — the VM's transfer since the start of the calendar month from Proxmox (`rrddata` netin/netout) into `UsageWatch` for the
`cloud` family; warn from 85 %, never cut off.

## What changed

- `ProxmoxComputeProvider::usage($vm, $periodStart)`: with a period, `transfer_bytes` = Σ (netin + netout) × step over the points of
  `GET /nodes/{node}/qemu/{vmid}/rrddata?timeframe=month&cf=AVERAGE` from the period start until now; a point without data is skipped,
  no data at all = `null` (not measured, never 0). Without a period nothing changes (no extra API call for the panel's live usage).
- `ServiceService::usage($service, $periodStart = null)` passes the period through.
- `UsageWatch::readings()`: a cloud service whose plan sells `traffic_tb` asks for the month (UTC calendar month) and gets the reading
  `transfer` against `traffic_tb × 1024⁴` (binary TB, like `traffic_gb`); a plan without it asks nothing more.
- `UsageMetrics`: metric `transfer` (registry `traffic_tb`, `bytes_month`, watched on no family → **new**: observed only until
  `ONHOST_METERING_ENFORCE_NEW_METRICS=true`, the D+30 step of P-R24). `MetricRegistry` `traffic_tb`: `measured`, `soft`, family
  `cloud`; `gap_families.addon` names the CDN add-on that still sells it unmeasured.
- Soft = tells, never blocks/throttles/orders (`drivesConsequences` false; `UsageGuard` and the auto-upgrade only act on hard metrics).
  `NotificationRouter` `service.usage.high` at level `full` for a soft top metric says the service is not limited or disconnected
  (the old text said nothing more can be added).
- `PlanPromisesTest` "GAP row for every KNOWN_GAPS key": a key kept for some families names its gap families with a reason.
