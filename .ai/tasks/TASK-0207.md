## Security considerations

Self-review (money + staff endpoint + export of personal data, ecc:security-reviewer style): authorization `billing.reconcile` at the global scope through ApiContext (role AND token scope) before anything is read; the route has no customer variant. The read is recorded in the staff trail (`staff.read.accounting_export`). The CSV carries buyer names/IČO/DIČ (needed by the accountant) and no secrets, no card data, no bank account numbers, no internal ids of organizations. CSV/formula injection: customer-controlled cells (names, references) cannot start a formula (apostrophe), separators and quotes are quoted (unit test). Response `no-store`, `nosniff`, attachment. Month input strictly validated by regex; no SQL from input (bounds are Carbon values bound as parameters). Read-only. No finding open.
