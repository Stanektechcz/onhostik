## Objective

A chargeback estimate for a given moment counts the same accounting (Europe/Prague) day as the withdrawal estimate and as every
other accounting computation, so the two estimates of one moment agree at any hour — and CI is green between 22:00 and 24:00 UTC.

## Context

Phase K, K0. CI of PR #153 (run 38179691547, ~22:20 UTC) failed on `pest`, `php85` and pest-postgres shard 3/4 with
`RefundEstimateTest > it estimates a consumer withdrawal …`: `Failed asserting that 191863 is identical to 192390.` It reproduced
locally at 22:4x UTC. `WithdrawalService::estimate()` takes the accounting day of the notice (`AccountingClock::date($asOf)`, Prague),
while `ChargebackService::estimate($service, $percent, $asOf)` took `$asOf->startOfDay()` — the UTC day. Between midnight Prague and
midnight UTC (22:00/23:00–24:00 UTC) the chargeback estimate counted one day fewer as used, so it promised a day more of refund than
the withdrawal of the same moment. Without `$asOf` it already used the accounting day (`AccountingClock::date()`), so the customer
estimate endpoint (`ServiceController`, no `$asOf`) was right; the withdrawal path passes a Prague date already (idempotent).
