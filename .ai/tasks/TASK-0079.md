## Objective

An organization owner can give a pipeline its own credential — a service account with an organization role and scoped tokens —
that belongs to the organization, is managed through the bus (HIGH, step-up) and stops at once when revoked. Tokens get a
`dns:read` scope, and the `/v1/domains/{zone}/zone` aliases ask what the canonical DNS routes ask.

## Security considerations

Owner-only check before dispatch and in the handler; HIGH + step-up via the bus (catalogue floor of `api_token.manage`);
routes have no TokenRouteScope family (no token manages tokens); account roles exclude owner/guest/svc_*/staff; SA tokens act
for their organization only, as actor `service_account`, never past a step-up; disabled/removed accounts and revoked tokens
are refused at the next request; the secret is masked in the replay store and the audit (Redactor by key fragment `token`).
