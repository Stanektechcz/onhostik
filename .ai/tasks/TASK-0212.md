## Objective

`/metrics` exports `onhost_schedule_last_success_seconds{command}` and `onhost_schedule_budget_seconds{command}` for every
scheduled job when PHP-FPM serves it, so the Prometheus alert `OnhostScheduledJobOverdue` (P12/P14) can fire.

## Context

Found in the staging deploy of `fda0cedf` (R3 step C4, 2026-10-13 21:25 UTC, protocol `staging-rehearsal-2026-10-17.md`):
`scheduled_job_runs` had 7–8 rows and the doctor said "92 jobs, every one within its budget", but `/metrics` printed only
`onhost_schedule_last_success_seconds{command="none"}`. The schedule lives in `routes/console.php`, which only the console
kernel loads; in PHP-FPM `app(Schedule::class)->events()` is empty. The P14 test passed because Pest boots the console kernel.
