## Objective

A partner's commission is paid once, for exactly what it earned, to the account the partner's owner confirmed — and only
after somebody other than the requester and the approver signed the payment. Partner portal reads need their own
permission, and the partner no longer sees its clients' contacts or who is overdue.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Two payout requests with different Idempotency-Keys racing for one balance → one payout; every payout amount =
      the sum of the commissions it allocated (computed under the partner row lock).
- [x] The IBAN comes only from the confirmed payout account; a different IBAN in the request is refused and nothing is
      written; an IBAN a paid payout went to is grandfathered; automatic payouts follow the same rule.
- [x] Changing the account: owner only, fresh step-up, notice mail to owner and billing contact, usable after 7 days,
      a pending change can be called off, check digits verified.
- [x] Paid only from `approved`, once, by somebody other than the requester and the approver, with a second person
      (CRITICAL); the sole operator after the time lock.
- [x] `onhost:partners:payout-anomalies` lists (dry run) and with `--apply` freezes only payouts above their allocation
      or to an IBAN that was not confirmed at the request; first payouts listed, not frozen; frozen payouts cannot be
      approved or paid, finance rejects or releases them with a reason.
- [x] Portal reads need `partner.portal.read`; client contacts and dunning masked in the partner view, kept for staff;
      `onhost:partners:masking-notice --send` tells each active partner once.

## Security considerations

Money out (SECURITY_RULES §3), permissions and catalogue (§1), personal data of clients (P5). The race is modelled on
one SQLite connection (the second request runs at the first commission read unless the first holds the partner row in
its own transaction; on PostgreSQL the model also requires `FOR UPDATE` in the SQL) — CI `pest-postgres` is the separate
evidence. Nothing prints or stores a whole IBAN outside the account/payout rows (events, audit, mails and CLI output
are masked; `PartnerPortalCommand::AUDIT_STRIP` strips `iban`).

## Migration considerations

`000890` adds one table and seven nullable columns; no data is written (grandfathering is read-time, owner rule).
Old code ignores the columns. Rollback drops them (`down()`), losing only account changes made after the deploy.

## Rollback

Revert the branch commit and roll back migration `000890`; re-run `AuthorizationSeeder` (the two permission keys
disappear). Payouts frozen by `--apply` would stay frozen in the column that `down()` drops — release them first
(`/unfreeze`) or accept that the rollback unfreezes them.
