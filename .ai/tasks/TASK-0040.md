## Objective

A partner's commission is paid once, for exactly what it earned, to the account the partner's owner confirmed — and only
after somebody other than the requester and the approver signed the payment. Partner portal reads need their own
permission, and the partner no longer sees its clients' contacts or who is overdue.

### Review round 1 (2026-09-27) — findings and resolutions

- **HIGH (billing + security) — an unverified IBAN could become the confirmed account.** Grandfathering had no cut-over
  (`of()` took the IBAN of the latest paid bank transfer, whenever paid) and `markPayoutPaid` never checked the
  destination, so a payout the old code left open (a first payout, only listed; an `iban_changed` one finance released;
  any before `--apply` ran) was paid to the IBAN its request typed and then became the account for every later payout,
  automatic ones included. **Resolved:** (1) migration `000890` records the cut-over once (`system_settings`
  `partners.payout_account.grandfathered_before`; no staff screen writes that key; missing → nothing grandfathered);
  `PayoutAccounts::of()` and the new `PayoutAccounts::confirmedAt()` grandfather only payouts paid before it.
  (2) `PartnerPayouts::assertAccountConfirmed()` in `markPayoutPaid`, under the row lock: a bank transfer is paid only to an
  IBAN in `confirmedAt(partner, requested_at)` (owner rows usable then, IBANs paid before the cut-over), and a payout naming
  `payout_account_id` only to exactly that row's IBAN (a release never overrides this); a leftover without an account row
  passes only after a recorded finance release (`partner.payout.unfreeze`, `result = succeeded`, the domain's own audit
  row — the bus's denied rows carry no resource id); otherwise 409 `payout_account_unconfirmed` (finance rejects, the owner
  sets the account, the partner asks again). `PayoutAnomalies` uses the same `confirmedAt()` and counts only succeeded
  releases. Both reviewers' resolutions combined: reviewer 1's cut-over and refusal, reviewer 3's "the only way through is
  a recorded release"; the set semantics (any IBAN confirmed at the request) keep the pay step and the scan in agreement.
  On "one finance person unfroze (NORMAL risk)": the command's own level is NORMAL, but the TASK-0037 risk floor raises it
  to `partner.manage`'s HIGH (step-up); the payment itself stays CRITICAL (a second person).
- **MEDIUM (billing) — `--apply` froze whatever a new scan found.** **Resolved:** the dry run prints `List digest: <12 hex>`
  (sha256 over payout id, state, amount, masked account and kinds, sorted); `--apply` needs `--digest=<it>` and freezes
  nothing (exit 1) without it or when the new scan's digest differs.
- **MEDIUM (qa) — no API-token test on the partner portal.** **Resolved:** a token with `TokenScopes::ALL` gets 403 on the
  five portal reads, `POST /v1/partner/payouts`, `PUT /v1/partner/payout-account` and `DELETE …/pending`; nothing written.
- **MEDIUM (qa) — no negative staff test.** **Resolved:** `support_l2`, `billing_operator`, `auditor_read_only` (no
  `partner.manage`), stepped up, get 403 `access_not_approved` on approve/reject/pay/freeze/unfreeze; state unchanged.
- **MEDIUM (qa) — the pay race was tested only sequentially.** **Resolved:** `pstPayRace` (the pay-side twin of `pstRace`):
  two payments of one approved payout, the second arriving at the first's commission read → one ledger transaction, the
  second `payout_not_approved`.
