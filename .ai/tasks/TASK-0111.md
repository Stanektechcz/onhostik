## Objective

Owner decision G-R1: only the invoice is a tax document. Paying an issued invoice (card, bank transfer, credit) settles it without a second document with VAT; a non-VAT-payer's top-up gets a non-tax payment confirmation; a read-only report lists historical invoice + receipt pairs.
