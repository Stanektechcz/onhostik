## Objective

The customer's ONhost account survives the end of their services (the owner's rule of 2026-09-24); erasing it is
possible only as the owner's deliberate, confirmed act, and can be stopped for a grace period.

## Context

Found by the investigation workflow's termination lens and verified by reading: `POST /v1/data-requests
{kind: deletion}` was authorised with `organization.manage` (every org admin), called the service directly (no
CommandBus, no step-up), and `onhost:compliance:data-requests` executed it within 30 minutes — every member
anonymised including the owner, the organization closed, the final archives of cancelled services erased. The
catalogue defines `organization.close` (CRITICAL, owner only) for exactly this and nothing asked for it.
`assertDeletable` ignored registered domains.

## Out of scope

  the platform created it, so it may clean it; a follow-up.
- An e-mail to the owner when the erasure is scheduled (in-app notification only; a mail template is a follow-up).

## Contracts affected

- `POST /v1/data-requests` now dispatches `DataRequestCommand` (same body and 202 response). An erasure needs
  `organization.close` + step-up. Without an `Idempotency-Key` header every request is a fresh one.
- New `POST /v1/data-requests/{id}/cancel` (`organization.manage`).
- New event `compliance.data_request.deletion_scheduled`; new config `onhost.compliance.deletion_grace_days`
  (`ONHOST_DELETION_GRACE_DAYS`, 14); new block `active_domains`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] An org admin cannot request the erasure (403).
- [x] The owner cannot without a fresh step-up (403 `step_up_required`).
- [x] The erasure waits the grace period; the scheduler leaves it alone meanwhile.
- [x] Any manager can cancel it; a cancelled erasure never runs.
- [x] After the grace period it runs.
- [x] An account holding a registered domain is not erased (`active_domains`).

## Security considerations

Four-eyes deliberately not required: the right to erasure cannot depend on the customer having a colleague (an
organization of one has nobody to approve). The step-up proves the owner is present; the grace period with
cancellation is the second look. AI actors are refused (risk HIGH).

## Migration considerations

None. Requests already in the table (none in production) without `execute_after` are processed as before.
