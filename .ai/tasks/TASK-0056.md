## Security considerations

- Holder change is HIGH (step-up), tenant-scoped through the domain, idempotent through the bus key; the audit row names fields, not values. A holder's identity (name, company, IČO, DIČ) can not be changed — it would be a transfer of the domain.
- FINDING (HIGH, not fixed here): `POST /v1/domains/transfer-in` charges nothing. `DomainService::transferIn` starts the saga without a wallet hold; `SubmitTransferStep` says the transfer renews the name "from our credit"; a cart line with `action: transfer` is priced by `QuoteService` but `createFromOrderItem` registers instead of transferring. A customer-triggered transfer therefore costs the platform a renewal and bills the customer nothing. The screen is built on the endpoint as it is; do not release it before a hold/capture/release like `renew()` (or the order path with the code collected after payment) exists.
