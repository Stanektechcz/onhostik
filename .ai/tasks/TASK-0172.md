## Objective

The M2 item "WAF `advanced+cdn` and the add-on 'CDN a WAF' vs the decision 'bez CDN' (2026-10-06)" of `PROMPT-faze-M-2026-10-12.md`.
Finding: the owner's "bez CDN" (H-R2) is about the platform's own origin (`TRUSTED_PROXIES`), not a customer add-on — the catalogue
is not changed for it. The real defect: the add-on `cdn` is delivered by the platform's Cloudflare connection, and without a configured
account (`onhost.cdn.cloudflare.secret_ref`) it was offered, sold and billed while delivering nothing. By owner decision R4 (a plan
offers only what its server delivers) it is now offered, quoted and paid only with that account; its page says "Připravujeme".

- `tests/Feature/Orders/CdnAvailabilityTest.php` (new); tests that sell CDN configure the account in `beforeEach`
  (`PricingAdminTest`, `PublicPagesSeamTest`, `G3RedeemTest`, `AddonDeliveryTest`, `PricingRulesTest`)

## Not changed

The Profi plan's WAF value `advanced+cdn` (WafLevels reads it as `advanced`; ServiceFeatures turns the CDN part on only with the
account). Whether the installation sells CDN at all is the owner's choice of a Cloudflare account (input for phase N).
