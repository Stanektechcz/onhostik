## Security considerations

Pure tightening: nothing widens. Staff roles gain three NORMAL read keys that duplicate what their customer keys already
reached. The time lock cannot be bypassed by approving one's own request (`approval_own_request`), is spent once (atomic
update), expires `approval_ttl_hours` after `not_before`, and requires a fresh step-up at the repeat. A token can never
step up, so token calls of the 61 raised operations are refused (listed by the dry-run first).

## Migration considerations

None (no schema change). `AuthorizationSeeder` on deploy adds the three keys and role rows by diff inside one transaction.

definitions rows stay, harmless). No data migration to undo; open time-locked approvals simply expire.
