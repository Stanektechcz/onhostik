## Objective

A managed database (and a VPS with a backup add-on) that is sold with backups gets scheduled backups with the sold
retention — but only once the owner switches the new automation rule `backups.compute` on. Until then nothing changes
for any service, and a read-only dry run shows the owner whom the rule would touch.

## Security considerations

- Destructive at a provider: deletion only of `kind = scheduled`, unprotected, non-final rows past retention or beyond the
  generation cap, not under legal hold (service or organization), whose `provider_instance_id` is the service's and whose volid
  names the service's own VMID; anything else is kept with a reason. `expireBackup` unprotects before deleting — used here only
  for rows this schedule made (never `protected` in our table).
- Only platform-provisioned servers (instance + binding) are selected; historical/foreign resources are not looked at.
- The customer schedule endpoint is unchanged: capped by the sold ceiling; for a VPS the ceiling comes from the ACTIVE add-on's
  own entitlements, so a policy row re-saved under the plan key cannot outlive a cancelled add-on.
- No secrets, events or payload keys added.

## Migration considerations

None (no schema change). The switch lives in the existing settings key `automation.enabled`.

## Rollback

Switch `backups.compute` off → the previous behaviour for every family (backups already made stay; nothing is deleted while
off). Code: revert the commit. The only behaviour change with the switch off is the new `final` guard in the prune (the
scheduler no longer deletes an unprotected final archive; `FinalArchive::prune()` still does at its retention).

## Findings

- MEDIUM `domains/Services/Web/BackupScheduler.php:52` (pre-existing) — `tick($limit = 100)` orders by id with a fixed limit, so a
  platform with more than 100 active web/managed/mail services never schedules the ones after the first 100. Not fixed (out of
  scope); compute services get a window of their own so this task does not make it worse.
- LOW (pre-existing, fixed here) — the scheduler's retention prune could delete an **unprotected** `final` archive of a live
  service (proven: the new web-final test fails with `'deleted'` when the guard is removed).
- NOTE — the VPS backup add-ons sell `offsite`; nothing copies a vzdump volume off-site (`offsite()` needs `backup_download`).
- NOTE — VDS plans sell `backup: daily 7d` (string) and the configurator's `backup` option sets `backup = daily`; no schedule
  reads them.
- NOTE — doctor queries (`app/Console/Commands/Doctor.php:176,206`) cover `web/managed/mail` only.
