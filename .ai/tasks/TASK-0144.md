## Objective

L-06: a consumer withdraws in the panel (or by letter) without having to agree to a refund to the credit; without the agreement
the money goes back the way it was paid. L-09: finance has a path to return a consumer's statutory money (defect, provider's
termination, undeliverable order) to the order's original payment instead of the credit.

## Context

`docs/legal/LEGAL_REVIEW_2026-10.md` L-06, L-09 (PR #138); § 1831 OZ, art. 11a of directive 2011/83/EU (2023/2673); owner decision
G-R4 (credit is not paid out) has a statutory exception in VOP 2026-10 čl. 4.3. Built on G6 (`OrderPaymentRefunds`).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A withdrawal without `confirm_refund_to_credit` is accepted (no consent row, `refund_method = source`).
- [x] Paid from the credit → back to the credit; paid by an order payment → held on the credit, `payout_due`, finance pays it out once.
- [x] The agreement to the credit stays possible; no payout then.
- [x] Statutory refund: from the document (credit note + payout) or from a credit note's returned credit; consumers only, ticket,
  services ended (except `defect`), never more than returned / available / left of the payment; top-up never.

## Security considerations

Self-check (money out of the company, SECURITY_RULES §1):
- Who: staff routes only, `billing.refund.execute`, fresh step-up; four eyes from `refund_approval_threshold`, re-checked under the payment lock.
- Amount: the payout is the withdrawal's own (never typed); the statutory amount is capped by what is left of the payment, of the
  document, of what the named credit note returned (less what moved before) and by the available credit.
- Double spend (MEDIUM, fixed): a withdrawal's own credit note cannot also be moved by the statutory path (`refund_credit_note_owned_by_withdrawal`, tested).
- Races: withdrawal row, payment, credit note and wallet are locked inside one transaction; idempotency key per refund.
- Customer side: choosing `source` only holds the customer's own returned money; nothing leaves without finance.
- Top-up: `topup_not_refundable` first; G4 guard test unchanged and green.
- Residual LOW: a hold that could not be placed (credit spent in the same instant) makes the payout refuse with `refund_exceeds_credit` — finance sees it.
