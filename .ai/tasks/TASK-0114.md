## Security considerations

Money path (SECURITY_RULES §3): points never become credit or cash (a credit note returns the paid money only, the points line

choice bound to the organization it was made for; quote never trusts a client amount (points are re-validated at quote and
placement). Needs onhost-security review (billing/orders).
