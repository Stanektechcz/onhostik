## Objective

**Package pay-and-restore (owner decision 23).** A service that ended — cancelled by dunning, deactivated because its
subscription ran out, or cancelled by the customer — comes back inside its restore window once what it owes is paid,
through the ordinary resume; an undone cancellation is billed again; a chargeback-cancelled service cannot be resumed by
the customer for free. Everything that reaches existing services sits behind the default-off rule `services.reinstate`.

**Package withdrawal (owner decision 17).** A consumer can withdraw from a distance contract within 14 days of the order:
the service is suspended first, then the paid, unused part comes back to the account credit as a credit note of the paid
document lines (`giveBack` → `returnToCredit`, never a top-up), then the service is cancelled through the normal bus
(terminate saga, final backup), and it cannot be resumed by the customer. Businesses (B2B at order time) and registered
domains are excluded; one order line is withdrawn once; everything is behind the default-off rule `billing.withdrawal`.

## Security considerations

pay-and-restore: spends the organization's credit → `billing.wallet.spend` at organization scope (guests of one service, org_admin and API tokens cannot); the handler refuses AI actors; one wallet key per cancellation plus a row lock (no double charge); money lifts only the `payment` hold (abuse/review/legal refused); the chargeback hole (resume after refund) closed unconditionally; the quote endpoint needs billing.wallet.read. onhost-security and billing review are mandatory.

withdrawal: ends a contract and moves money → `service.delete` at organization scope, HIGH, fresh step-up (a guest of one service, API tokens and assistants cannot; the handler refuses AI actors); the staff record is `billing.refund.execute`, CRITICAL (step-up + four-eyes) because staff act without the consumer's step-up and may date the notice back (never into the future, never before the order). The service is resolved inside the command's organization (404/403 across tenants). The refund is computed from the paid document lines fixed at the notice and capped at what each line has left when the credit note is made; `giveBack` returns only what was really paid (unpaid documents are reduced). One row per order line (`subject_key` unique) under the service row lock; the refund once under the withdrawal row lock; service operations run as the platform with fixed keys. The `withdrawal` hold blocks the customer's resume and pay-and-restore; staff can still resume with a reason (the refund stays — a deliberate decision). Event payloads carry no secrets (keys checked against the outbox redactor). onhost-security and billing review are mandatory.
