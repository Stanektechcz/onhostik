## Objective

**Package pay-and-restore (owner decision 23).** A service that ended — cancelled by dunning, deactivated because its
subscription ran out, or cancelled by the customer — comes back inside its restore window once what it owes is paid,
through the ordinary resume; an undone cancellation is billed again; a chargeback-cancelled service cannot be resumed by
the customer for free. Everything that reaches existing services sits behind the default-off rule `services.reinstate`.

`billing.wallet.spend` (TASK-0021 contract, added identically here), chargeback-cancelled service not resumable by the
customer (side finding fixed here), purge cancellation part of the restore, default_off for anything reaching existing

## Acceptance criteria

pay-and-restore:
- [x] A paid invoice of a TERMINATED dunning case restores the service in its window when the paid period covers it (no charge), rule on.
- [x] `GET /v1/services/{id}/reinstatement` quote; `POST /v1/services/{id}/reinstate` charges one period from the credit (statement `meta.reinstatement`), calls the purge off, resumes through the bus lifting only `payment`.
- [x] Short credit records the wish (`awaiting_payment` + shortfall); the next top-up restores with exactly one charge; a top-up alone never charges an un-asked service.
- [x] An undone own cancellation restarts billing (covered period runs on, auto-renew as before); after the period ended the customer resume answers 402 with the quote; rule off → as before.
- [x] Chargeback-cancelled service: customer resume refused (409 `chargeback_cancelled` rule off, 402 rule on); staff resume restarts billing from today.
- [x] Abuse/staff hold, window over, purged service → refused, nothing charged; carried sites of a reinstated parent are not purged (`parent_reinstated`).
- [x] Idempotent (same key → one charge, one resume op; other key → 409 `not_cancelled`); renewal day re-anchored; `billing.wallet.spend` required (org_admin 403, billing_admin 202).
- [x] `onhost:billing:reinstatement-audit` read-only; `--apply` only with one `--service` from the unbilled list.

## Security considerations

pay-and-restore: spends the organization's credit → `billing.wallet.spend` at organization scope (guests of one service, org_admin and API tokens cannot); the handler refuses AI actors; one wallet key per cancellation plus a row lock (no double charge); money lifts only the `payment` hold (abuse/review/legal refused); the chargeback hole (resume after refund) closed unconditionally; the quote endpoint needs billing.wallet.read. onhost-security and billing review are mandatory.

## Migration considerations

pay-and-restore: none (services.tags JSON, settings table).

## Rollback

pay-and-restore: switch the rule `services.reinstate` off (everything new stops, except the chargeback resume refusal, the honest payment-hold message for cancelled services and the `deletion.subscription` record, which are harmless). Code rollback = revert the commit; no data migration. Statements/charges already made stay (append-only); a wrong one is corrected with a credit note.
