## Objective

Phase V, SP-1 answered by the owner (V-R1): the first package of the automated antispam — research and architecture
(`docs/proposals/antispam-reputation.md`) and a reputation monitor that asks a curated blocklist catalogue about every sending
address (IPv4 + IPv6 of every node, extra addresses from config) and every customer mail domain, through the operator's own
resolver, with history of listings, doctor rows and metrics.

## Context

The old `BlocklistCheck` asked 3 lists daily through the system resolver and treated **every** `127.x` answer as a listing —
Spamhaus answers `127.255.255.254` to every query through a public resolver (verified 2026-10-14 against 1.1.1.1), and the staging
host's resolver is `8.8.8.8`: once staging has nodes with an address, every node would have been "listed on ZEN" every day. No
IPv6, no domains, no clear, no history.
