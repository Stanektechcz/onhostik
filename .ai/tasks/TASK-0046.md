## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A token bound to A: `GET /v1/me` lists A only; nothing of B (id, name, billing e-mail, ICO, address) is in the answer.
- [x] A token bound to no organization: the organization acted for in full, every other one `{id, name}` only.
- [x] The portal session keeps every membership in full.
- [x] A global staff binding is refused on each customer door separately, 404 `not_found`: backup_dr_admin on the archive
      endpoint, platform_owner on the archive endpoint and on the action endpoint; no operation, no fee waived. (backup_dr_admin
      never passed the action endpoint — it holds no `service.read`, 403 there before and after; pinned.)
- [x] backup_dr_admin restores in staff mode (`/v1/staff/services/{id}/actions`, 202); a member of staff who is org_admin of the
      organization restores there on the customer route; the owner restores as before.
- [x] A staff role without the restore (support_l2) is refused in staff mode, nothing started.
- [x] A member of staff who is org_admin of the organization, on the staff route without staff keys: after the second person
      (own organization) the restore is 404 `not_found`; on the customer route the same person restores as org_admin.
- [x] The spec apply, action hook and Discord gates carry no hard-coded `service.manage` and use `ServiceActionCommand`.
- [x] `assertMayRestore` decides a token-sent restore on the token's view (row 133 LOW) — **defence in depth behind the bus**:
      at HTTP the bus decides first on the same view (`IdentityCommandAuthorizer::asToken`), so no request reaches the service
      with a gone token. Pinned at service level: a revoked token, another person's token and `token:abc` are refused 403
      `access_not_approved`, the owner's own bound token restores (the unfixed code asked the person and allowed all three).

## Security considerations

- No new permission, no catalogue change. Customer path: `Authorizer::customerPermissionsAt` (organization, project and resource
  bindings only, token-narrowed, inactive principal → nothing) — it writes no shadow row, because it never allows staff reach.
- Staff path: `StaffActor::acts` (staff mode = `/v1/staff/*`, a person acting for themselves, active staff account) + `StaffActor::may`
  (a global staff binding or a live global JIT elevation of a staff role carrying the key). The bus in front still asks
  `backup.restore` (staff reach, shadow-logged) — see handoff: once `ONHOST_STAFF_REACH_ENFORCED` is on, the staff route refuses
  archive restores at the bus until P0-15 gives the restore a staff key.
- Token view: `IdentityCommandAuthorizer::asToken` (read-only use; revoked/expired/foreign token → refused).
- `/v1/me`: the token is `TokenScopes::tokenOf`; the bound organization is the one `ApiContext::organization` already resolved
  (a token naming another is 403 `token_organization_mismatch` before this code).
