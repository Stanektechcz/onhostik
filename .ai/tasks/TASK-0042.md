## Objective

Every way of giving, changing or taking away access in a customer organization is decided by one GrantPolicy with the
program's invariants I1–I12, and whatever is taken away can be given back exactly: an access snapshot precedes every removal
or role change, ownership moves only when the heir accepts, and a customer owner who lost access is recovered by a
cancellable, time-delayed command instead of an ad-hoc MFA reset.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Every grant entry point × every invariant I1–I12 is either proven by a test or has a stated reason (GrantMatrixTest).
- [x] A demoted or removed inviter's pending invitation cannot be accepted; acceptance runs through the bus and re-checks the inviter.
- [x] Remove + restore returns identical access (organization role, `access_until`, project roles, `svc_*` bindings, shares).
- [x] Grantor-loss cascade: pending grants cancelled; active grants recorded with the switch off (dry-run lists them), revoked with it on.
- [x] Ownership moves only after the heir accepts; cancellable by the owner, declinable by the heir; both are told.
- [x] Owner recovery: CRITICAL (second person), at least 7 days of notice to every member and the owner, cancellable by any
      org_admin, token/export/ownership held meanwhile; `iam.mfa.reset` on a customer owner without it is refused.

## Security considerations

New refusals only for illegitimate grants: a share narrowed or revoked by somebody who could not give it (I3), a share chain
deeper than two (I12), a customer role for a staff account (I11), a token for a non-member (I8), an ownership offer to oneself,
an MFA reset of a customer owner outside the recovery. Clamps, not refusals, for I5.

## Migration considerations

One additive migration (three new tables); no existing row is changed. The cascade switch is default off.

## Rollback

Revert the commits; drop the three tables (`down()`); no data outside them is written by the new code paths except the
`granted_by` of new bindings (now the grantor instead of the clicker) and project-membership rows deleted with a removal.

Test: "asks a second person before…". |
