documents print, whatever hour a document was issued or a scenario runs; a 24-hour clock sweep proves it and keeps it so.

## Diagnosis

Sweeps run with `tests/Support/ClockSweep.php`: every scenario at every step of a local day (UTC "tomorrow", and a Prague
winter day for the +1 h offset), each run in its own savepoint, at the hour production runs the job.

1. **Product bug — overdue mark on the printed due date.** `InvoiceService::overdueSweep()` compared instants:
   `due_at < now()` at 01:15 UTC. The PDF prints the due date as the Prague day of `due_at`
   (`invoice.blade.php`, `UblExporter` via `AccountingClock`). Every invoice due between Prague midnight and 01:15 UTC —
   i.e. issued in that window 14 days earlier, which includes the renewal runs of 22:20, 23:20 and 00:20 UTC in summer
   (23:20, 00:20 in winter) — was marked OVERDUE, and the customer mailed "Doklad … je po splatnosti", on its own due date
   with the day still to pay. Base-code sweep (red): UTC day 00:00–01:00 ×5 and 22:00–23:45 ×8; Prague winter day
   00:00–02:00 local (23:00–01:00 UTC) ×9.
2. **Product bug — dunning counted UTC days.** `DunningService::tick()` counted `due_at->startOfDay()` to `now()->startOfDay()`
   in UTC. A case due at 22:00–24:00 UTC (summer; 23:00–24:00 winter) is due the NEXT day in Prague, so every reminder,
   grace, the suspension/limit and the termination schedule came one day early. Base-code sweep (red): three-day reminder
   on the second printed day for due instants 22:00–23:45 UTC ×8 (winter 00:00–00:45 Prague ×4); the limit on printed day 29
   for 22:00–23:30 UTC ×4 (30-minute steps).
3. **Test artefact — WithdrawalTest.** `WithdrawalPolicy` counts the fourteen days and `ChargebackService` the unused days in
   the accounting calendar (correct: the contract day is the Prague day). Two tests built their expected dates from the UTC
   day (`now()->subDays(4)->addDays(14)->toDateString()`, a letter "sent" `now()->subDays(2)` at 12:00 UTC). Red at
   22:00–23:00 UTC (summer) and 00:00 Prague (winter): deadline `…-09` vs expected `…-08`; refund 19360 vs 18150. Fixed in
   the tests (`AccountingClock::now()`), product unchanged.
4. **Test artefact — LimitRaiseTest, already fixed in the base by e104507.** The yearly-raise case travelled one flat day
   after a dunning tick; `DunningService` looks at a case again at tomorrow 06:00 UTC (production runs it daily at 06:00),
   so a start between 00:00 and 06:00 UTC landed before it. e104507 (not in f55a6d8, which the release record gated) moved
   the travel to 07:00. Sweep with the pre-e104507 line put back: red 00:00–05:00 UTC ×6 (hourly); with the base line:
   green at every hour. A frozen-clock run of LimitRaiseTest + WithdrawalTest at 00:36, 01:28, 02:02, 03:50, 05:40 UTC:
   65/65; at 22:30, 23:10, 23:30 UTC: only the two WithdrawalTest cases above failed.
5. **Renewals (hourly :20):** swept (service activated at every hour, UTC tomorrow and a Prague 31st of January): charged
   once per due run, not an hour early, the full price, second period starting the accounting day after the first — green
   on the base code; no change.

6. **DST days (review round 1, MEDIUM 1).** `clockSweep()` now steps real elapsed time from local midnight to the next
   local midnight: the last Sunday of March has 92 quarter-hours, the last Sunday of October 100 (the repeated hour twice,
   labels carry the UTC time). The overdue and dunning sweeps run on the next of both days — issued ON the day, and issued
   fourteen days before so that the due date and the overdue run fall on it and the dunning days are counted across the
   change — and assert the number of instants visited. Base product code put back for one run (restored from HEAD
   afterwards): red on all four DST datasets of both sweeps, e.g. overdue *due on the 25-hour day* 00:00–03:00 local ×13,
   *issued on the 23-hour day* 00:00–01:45 local ×8 + 03:00 ×1; dunning *due on the 25-hour day* 00:00–01:45 local ×8.
   Green with the fix (15/15 in BillingClockSweepTest).
7. **Domain expiry days (review round 1, MEDIUM 2) — product bug, customer-visible.** Registrars give the expiry as a DATE
   (WEDOS `expiration`, Subreg `exDate` cut to Y-m-d), stored at midnight (app zone) and printed as that date next to
   "expiruje za N dní" (panel, API `days_to_expiry`, renewal bucket). `Domain::daysToExpiry()` counted from the UTC day,
   so from midnight to 01:00/02:00 Prague N was one too many. Sweep on the base code (red): the count ×8 at 22:00–23:45 UTC,
   ×4 at 00:00–00:45 Prague winter, ×4 on the 23-hour day, ×8 on the 25-hour day; the seven-day notice was sent as the
   fourteen-day one (`days` 14, `days_left` 8) at 22:00–23:00 UTC and 00:00 Prague winter (the right notice followed at
   00:00 UTC, i.e. 02:00 Prague — same day, two hours late, wrong wording first); `domain.renewal_payment_failed` said
   `days_left` 1 on the printed expiry day at 22:00–23:00 UTC. Not changed by the mismatch: the renewal retry/abandon
   decision (`daysLeft >= 1 || sinceExpiry <= grace`: on the expiry day `sinceExpiry` is 0, so it retries either way — pinned
   by the sweep, retried at every hour). Fixed: the count goes from `AccountingClock`'s day to the printed date.
   `in_grace` ("Doména už expirovala") now reads the registry expiry instant (`! expires_at->isFuture()`) instead of
   `daysLeft < 1`, which was the same thing for midnight-stored dates under UTC days and would otherwise have said
   "expired" for the one or two hours before the stored expiry. `RegistrarConnectionService::notices()` (TASK-0045's file,
   not touched) reads the same count: its notices now come at Prague midnight with the calendar count; nothing to change
   there. `RegistrarConnectionTest` built its expiry from the UTC day and failed at 22:08 UTC once the count was right —
   fixture now in `AccountingClock` days; green at 22:11 UTC.

Root cause in one line: jobs compared UTC instants and counted UTC days where the documents state Prague accounting days.

## Changes

- `AccountingClock::daysBetween()` (whole accounting days) and `AccountingClock::startOfToday()` (start of the Prague day as
  an instant in the application zone).
- `InvoiceService::overdueSweep()`: `due_at < AccountingClock::startOfToday()` — overdue once the printed due date is over.
- `DunningService::tick()`: `$days = AccountingClock::daysBetween($case->due_at)`.
- `OutboxPublisher::relayPending()`: the "inside a transaction" guard asks the testing transaction manager in tests
  (as `Authorizer::afterTransaction` already does); production path unchanged (`DB::transactionLevel() > 0`). Needed so a
  sweep run in a savepoint relays its events like a normal test.
- `tests/Support/ClockSweep.php`: `clockSweep()`, `clockSweepAt()`, `clockSweepWindows()`, `clockSweepNextDay()`,
  `clockSweepLabel()`, `clockSweepRunAt()`, `clockSweepSavepointManager()`, `clockSweepWhere()` (required per file, not
  autoloaded; no change to `tests/Pest.php`).

  day 30, renewals ×2); `WithdrawalTest` two expectations in accounting days, two scenarios extracted to bindable closures,
  two sweeps; `LimitRaiseTest` two dunning scenarios extracted, one sweep.
